Owner requirement (chat, 2026-11-17, two messages): (1) "we need a new field state - human or ai review - that it is clear, this needs to be done by a human" + "if I need to test something, it would be in the last stage of the review". (2) Clarification of the mental model: "for each stage in the v model, we do have tasks and tickets ... in the first stage, it can happen it is blocked and needs to be resolved by a human (ai can not further execute and it escalates to this stage). also, on the last stage, it could be in review, however, needs to be done by a human, as ai can not validate."

Distilled: ONE per-ticket field, `review: human | ai`, that answers "who must act for this ticket to move on" - visible at every V stage:
- FIRST-stage shape (requirements): the AI cannot further execute -> the ticket is blocked and escalates for a HUMAN decision/clarification (today's NEEDS-HUMAN mechanics, now explicitly marked `review: human` so 'needs a human' is a field, not an inference from free text).
- LAST-stage shape (test-requirements / review): the ticket is `in-review` but the AI cannot validate (live test, acceptance) -> `review: human` means only a HUMAN verdict moves it; the engine's review step must skip it.
- `review: ai` (or absent = backward-compatible default) keeps today's behavior end to end: engine verdicts, auto-accept, tickers.
- Owner testing stays at the last stage of the review leg (test-requirements, the T-039 standing rule).

Store discipline: persisted like resume_to (frontmatter round-trip), validated loudly at the verb (U-107/U-109 pattern: reject, never silently sanitize), no provider/model ids (D-004).
