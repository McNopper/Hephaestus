The design half of T-180's feature: ActiveSession - a pure resolver (newest time.updated wins; blank/no-time junk never sorts first) - and ChatView.restorePreviousSession wiring: active-scoped resolution first, remembered U-039 id as fallback (probe+clear semantics unchanged), fresh session last; secondary/new-chat views exempt; distinct notices per path so the resolution is observable. Chosen over alternatives: keep-lastSessionId-only (rejected - that IS the divergence bug) and service-side pinning (rejected - sessions are global, the client must resolve).
