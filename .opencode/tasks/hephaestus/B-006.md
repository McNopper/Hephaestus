Live 2026-09-19: four tickets blocked at stage boundaries (U-021 test-implementation, U-027/028/029 system) purely on stale branches from their previous stage runs. The V-model re-dispatches the same ticket per stage, so any settle residue stalls the chain - 'a lot of tickets getting blocked' trace to this. fleet_reset is the manual cure; the engine must do it itself.
