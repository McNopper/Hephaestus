Three occurrences in two days (2026-09-21 x2 tasks+fleet, 2026-09-22 tasks): the tasks/fleet stdio MCP servers report Connection closed and vanish from the tool catalog while the launcher JVM process is still running. Recovery works via POST /api/experimental/mcp/{server}/connect. Suspected: stdin/stdout pipe breaks (service restart, config reload, or jar replacement under the running JVM). Find the trigger; at minimum detect the dead pipe in the launcher and exit nonzero so opencode respawns instead of showing a zombie.
