REWORK child of A-001 (basis A-001, epic U-111 inherits) - escalation from paired verification A-002 (test-system, engine verdict FAIL). The system definition (docs/acceptance-run-system.md) promises every verb writes a history event WITH ITS ACTOR; the live store's status transitions ('updated:status') are actor-less, making the D-3 oracle ('done by the engine review') store-undecidable (T-031, A-003 evidence in the A-002 blocker). Scope: decide and specify ONE of (a) the store/engine stamps the acting agent on status transitions (change the engine, update the definition to match); or (b) re-specify the attribution promise and the D-3 evidence basis to what the store records: the engine-verdict COMMENT as the engine's mark (the T-031 pattern, now applied by the ticker on every PASS flip), in-review->done ordering, and an explicit rule for legacy actor-less dones. Then docs/acceptance-run-system.tests.md IT-A-102 (and IT-A-101's actor wording) is updated by the A-002 re-verification to the settled basis. Surface if the decision needs the owner (engine change = code lane, needs a requirements ticket).
