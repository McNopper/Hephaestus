opencode persists sessions server-side, and the steering route (POST /session/:id/prompt with delivery "steer", probe-verified in U-092) can re-enter an existing session. Today the fleet ALWAYS re-dispatches a fresh session on retry: a dead worker's conversation context is lost to the replacement, and recovery means re-reading ticket artifacts from scratch (the manual U-096 recovery consumed a full session of context doing exactly that). Resuming the existing session is checkpoint-lite built entirely from parts we already own - LangGraph's durable-execution insight without building a checkpointer (their lesson: the durable unit needs to carry context; ours already does, server-side).

Replay-safety is free in our architecture: worker edits are git-tracked in the worktree, and the central gate runs after every landing regardless of how the session got there.
