U-107 review note (non-blocking, backlog): nextId (TaskStore.java ~2103-2116) filters the prefix with replaceAll("[^A-Za-z0-9_-]", "") AFTER U-107's guard validates the raw value. A garbage prefix that passes the guard can re-compose the C--001 shape: 'W-!' strips to 'W-' -> mints 'W--001'; '!!' strips to empty -> silently falls back to 'T'. Full class closure: validate the POST-FILTER prefix at the single mint point (blank or trailing '-' after filtering => Invalid), and decide whether the silent '!!'->T fallback should also become loud. 1sp; fold the U-107 nit about the task_create summary wording ('(status=product-backlog)' imprecise for born-blocked tickets) into the same touch.
