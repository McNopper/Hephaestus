The forcing mechanism for rule 1/3: a definition stage cannot be exited without its artifact.

- task_advance out of requirements/system/architecture/design rejects when the ticket has zero recorded
  artifacts, with a message naming task_add_artifact (a pass-through/rationale recorded as a doc artifact counts).
- Same advance succeeds with >=1 artifact. implementation/test-* stage exits unchanged.
- task_doctor additionally reports done/in-review definition-stage tickets that carry no artifact (legacy rows),
  without failing parse - this is the 'architecture was not done' detective for pre-existing work.
