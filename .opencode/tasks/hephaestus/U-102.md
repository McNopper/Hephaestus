Adopt-and-remove per the revised experimental policy (2026-10-29). The Board's TaskStoreWatcher (local ReadStreamWatcher + 2s poll fallback, our code) watches .opencode/tasks for ticket changes. Upstream 2.2.0 added experimental fs.watch/unwatch (POST/DELETE /api/experimental/fs/watch) - server-side file watching over the same project filesystem. Evaluate whether it covers the Board's needs; if yes, the local watcher is removed and the Board reacts to server events instead. GUARD: the local watcher works even when the opencode service is down (store is local files) - a removal must not make the Board depend on a live service for local state, or the difference must be accepted explicitly by the owner.
