Live evidence 2026-09-15 (~03:51-04:03 UTC, engine run 188d9bbb): five tickets dispatched in one burst; within ~1 minute of each launch the engine recorded "worker produced no changes (no commits on opencode/<id> and no pending worktree edits)", marked the tickets blocked and released them, fleet_jobs showed FAILED - while the opencode log proves every session kept streaming on glm-5.3 (C--001 step 35, O-002 step 39 at 04:03Z, O-002 actively editing FleetToolProvider.java, worktree holding 6 modified files). I.e. the engine's session-completion detection fired prematurely (likely misattributed SSE completion from the burst of 5 concurrent sessions on the shared spawned server), verification then correctly saw no commits 1 minute in, and the failure path released the tickets but left the worker sessions running headless. Consequences: false FAILED state, orphaned productive sessions nobody will merge back, and a hazard that re-dispatch/reset destroys live work (fleet_reset would delete worktrees being written).
