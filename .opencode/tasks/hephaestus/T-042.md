Owner requirement 2026-11-15 (general operating model): in general the harness runs until the overall Product Goal is reached (ROADMAP Vision). If a human is reviewing and accepts, we create a release (refines T-041). The spectrum has two extremes: (A) the harness works for a specific amount of time and the result is the final product - max autonomy, human absent, time-boxed; (B) a human checks very often and gives constant feedback - max human involvement, tight loop. Every run sits somewhere on this spectrum; the owner sets the position via the goal horizon (time-boxed vs product-goal) and the review cadence (releases only on human review - T-041; recurring acceptance hardens without gating - T-039; NEEDS-HUMAN threshold). Fulfillment: land the spectrum in the doctrine - AGENTS.md autonomy-target section and the ROADMAP Product Goal paragraph state it as the operating model; the goal-intake semantics (U-105) expose the two dials (horizon, review cadence).
