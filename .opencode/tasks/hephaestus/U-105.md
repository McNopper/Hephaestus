Owner direction 2026-10-30: upstream 2.2.1's /goal <objective> (#59437 - the session keeps working until a reviewer confirms the objective; /goal pause|resume|clear) is ADOPTED as an entry point - with the decisive difference that goal fulfillment follows the V-MODEL, not a session grading itself. Upstream's built-in reviewer is the session's own self-review (independence violation by our standards); ours is the engine's review pass (U-021) against stage artifacts and the quality gate. Flow: (1) INTAKE - a goal stated by the owner is captured as a requirement ticket (objective verbatim as the definition artifact); (2) GOVERNED FULFILLMENT - decomposition along epic links, stages, paired verification, artifacts - dispatch through the fleet (isolation, budget, watchdog); (3) VERDICT - the goal is done ONLY when the pipeline's gate verdict lands done; the working session's own 'I think we're done' is never accepted. The upstream /goal session-primitive (pause/resume/clear) maps to store semantics: pause -> ticket paused, resume -> back to the pump, clear -> close or split, pipeline state preserved. Companion tickets: U-104 (chains keep the goal session alive through provider deaths), U-099 (retry resumes context). Pairs with the experimental policy: /goal is an overlap surface (semantic overlap with the V-loop) -> adopted and governed.
