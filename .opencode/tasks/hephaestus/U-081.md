Rule 4 in StageReadiness: proof of upstream work is the artifact, not the label.

A done/in-review epic-chain upstream ticket with ZERO recorded artifacts must not satisfy downstream -
downstream reports WAIT_UPSTREAM with a reason naming the upstream ('claims done but has no artifact').
Backfilling the artifact on the upstream unblocks it (forcing, cheap, honest).
Everything else stays: an upstream WITH an artifact satisfies as before (READY/STALE), own-advance-history
satisfaction unchanged, mid-pipeline entry verdicts unchanged. StageReadiness stays a pure function over
tickets (artifacts ride on the tickets - no file I/O).
