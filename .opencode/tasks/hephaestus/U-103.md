Adopt-and-remove evaluation per the revised experimental policy. Upstream 2.2.1 added experimental.schedule (6 routes): a schedule is {sessionID, name?, when (at/cron/every/in), ends?, prompt} bound to an EXISTING session - server-side recurring prompts, disabled by default behind experimental.schedules. It is a per-session TIMER PRIMITIVE - it cannot replace RecurringWaves (which plans waves from the ticket backlog with worktree isolation, budget admission, watchdog and the resolution pump). What it COULD replace is the pump's in-host tick: a schedule firing 'plan and drain the next wave' into the host session would make the autonomy loop survive host restarts - DETACHED operation. That is exactly the unattended-spend pattern our governance refuses TODAY: it is acceptable ONLY after U-037 (live auto-dispatch acceptance) proves the loop and the owner accepts detached firing with a hard budget. This ticket is therefore BLOCKED on U-037's acceptance, and its outcome is a decision ticket: adopt schedules as the trigger (removing our in-host tick), or close with the governance reason recorded.
